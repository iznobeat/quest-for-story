# Background atlas

File: `castle-atlas.png`
Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Original character sprites are drawn separately in Canvas code.

Prompt:
Create a production game background atlas, a single image divided into a precise 2 by 2 grid of FOUR different wide landscape scenes of identical dimensions, no gutters. Original 1990s point-and-click adventure pixel art, gorgeous detailed hand-pixelled VGA style, limited rich palette, moody indigo twilight, warm amber windows, teal foliage, distinctly visible crisp pixels, whimsical crooked architecture, nostalgic adventure game atmosphere. NO text, NO characters, NO UI. Top left: exterior courtyard of a sprawling eccentric Norwegian medieval castle at twilight, large castle wall and warm lit arched doorway on left half, cobbled ground lower quarter, small wooden well at center-right, wooden signpost far right, moon and mountains and pine silhouettes in distance. Top right: magnificent Amiga department in a huge castle library, warm candlelit stone arches, rainbow banners, many beige retro computers on desks at left and right, tall shelves, open walking area along bottom. Bottom left: absurdly tiny ominous DOS broom cupboard with green CRT terminal on desk toward right, floppy disks, buckets, mops, tangled cables, dark stone walls, spooky green light, open floor bottom quarter. Bottom right: castle newsroom observatory at night, wooden editor desk with beige computer on right, tall arched windows overlooking moonlit fjord, papers and books, small printing press left, warm lamps. Every quadrant is a polished cohesive playable adventure background, readable silhouettes, detailed textures, no gradients pretending to be pixels. Wide image overall, 3:2 aspect ratio.
