# Research and fiction

The new plot, Last Byte demo, castle exhibition, AUTOEXEC threat, disputed R.G.P. credit and all dialogue are original fiction. In particular, the game does not claim to identify Haj as Retrogamingpappa or to solve the real Twintris C64 mystery.

Historical details were checked against Spillhistorie.no:

- [The story of the Amiga cult classic Twintris](https://spillhistorie.no/2024/11/27/the-story-of-the-amiga-cult-classic-twintris/) — published 27 November 2024; Joachim Froholt and Retrogamingpappa. Supports the 1990 release, developer Svein Berge, composer Tor Gausen (Walkman), the distinction from Svein Berge of Röyksopp, and Martin Gjesdal’s recollection of Twintris tournaments. These facts appear briefly, paraphrased, on the archive clipping and in conversations. The real manual’s unexplained C64 listing helped inspire the fictional lost-credits mystery; no fictional resolution is attributed to that article.
- [Interview with Jon Hare of Sensible Software](https://spillhistorie.no/2025/02/21/interview-with-jon-hare-of-sensible-software/) — published 21 February 2025; Retrogamingpappa with help from Joachim Froholt. Provides context for the site’s collaborative interview work; the dialogue’s chronology joke is original, not an actual quote.

All soundtrack melodies and arrangements are original. No existing game music or interview audio is reproduced.

## Episode 02 — cd SPILL studio

The user supplied the [Test Drive III WebVTT transcript](https://s325.podbean.com/pb/7337b03d57199a05f32c1ed0db644075/6a9fd553/data4/fs127/7653866/uploads/TestDrive3.vtt?pbss=54478254-4aa5-5c14-9ed6-b45fd618fa7c), retrieved September 8, 2026. It is used as the source for the episode’s themes, rather than as verbatim game dialogue. This signed link may expire. The transcript is not bundled with the game.

Relevant discussion: Test Drive III: The Passion, open-world exploration, difficult steering, F6 returning the player to the road, Mamen’s water-edge respawns, Sigve’s explanation of manual reverse, the Pacific-to-Yosemite route, and both hosts appreciating the ambition while finding the driving frustrating. The rescue console adapts these anecdotes into a fictional puzzle; it is not an emulator or a claim about the actual game’s internal recording machinery. The closing verdict preserves the hosts’ distinction between technical admiration and how well the game plays today. Automatic transcription contains errors; incidental hardware claims were not used.

## Episode 03 — Leisure Suit Joachim

An original affectionate parody of the neon lounge, hapless flirtation, inventory logic and narrator humour of classic Leisure Suit Larry adventures. Joachim remains the hero. Civilization game boxes are the fictional eligible dates; there is no copied Larry dialogue, soundtrack or game background.

The user assigned the character preferences: original Civilization for Terje, V for Kiiim, VI for Izno, VII for Nikolai, and Sid Meier’s Pirates! for Haj. These are scenario roles, not researched statements about the real people. All interviews, dates, denials and jokes are newly authored fiction. The player chooses their personal favourite rather than receiving a universal factual ranking.
